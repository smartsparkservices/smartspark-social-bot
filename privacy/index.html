Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026 · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what data SmartSpark ("SmartSpark", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. SmartSpark is operated by SmartSpark Services, Arizona, USA. Questions go to info@smartsparkservices.com.

In short:

  • We use your data only to provide SmartSpark: creating, publishing and measuring your Instagram content and answering your customers on your behalf.
  • We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share data obtained from Instagram with data brokers or anyone else except the service providers needed to run SmartSpark.
  • Instagram access tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect or delete everything at any time (how).

1. Who this policy covers

  • Business users: people who sign up for SmartSpark and connect their business's Instagram professional account. For their account data we are the data controller.
  • Their customers: people who comment on, or send direct messages to, an Instagram account connected to SmartSpark. We process this data on behalf of that business (as its processor), under the business's instructions. The business is responsible for its own use of it.

2. Data we collect

From business users, directly

  • Account data: name, email address, and password (stored only as a salted hash; we never store the password itself).
  • Business data: business name, description, location and service areas, products, prices, availability, delivery terms, FAQs, brand voice and business rules.
  • Product photos you upload.
  • Notification contact: the WhatsApp number where you want to receive handoff alerts.
  • Technical data: sign-in times, IP address and browser user agent for security and session management.

Generated by SmartSpark

  • Content plans, captions, generated images and videos, validation results, and records of what was published and when.

3. Data from Instagram

When a business user connects an Instagram professional account through Instagram's official login, SmartSpark asks for these permissions and receives only the data they allow:

PermissionWhat we receiveWhy
instagram_business_basic Account ID, username, account type, and the account's own posts. Identify the connected account and confirm what was published.
instagram_business_content_publish Ability to publish posts, carousels, Reels and stories. Publish the content the business has scheduled.
instagram_business_manage_comments Comments on the account's posts: commenter's Instagram ID and username, comment text and time. Answer customer questions from the business's facts, or pass them to the owner.
instagram_business_manage_messages Direct messages sent to the account: sender's Instagram-scoped ID, message text and time. Answer customer messages, or pass them to the owner.
instagram_business_manage_insights (if granted) Post metrics such as reach, likes, comments, saves and shares. Show performance and improve future content plans.

We also store the access token Instagram issues so SmartSpark can act for the account. We use Instagram data only to provide SmartSpark to the business that connected the account. We do not use it to build profiles of individuals, for advertising, or for any purpose unrelated to that business's account, and we comply with the Meta Platform Terms.

4. How we use data

  • To provide SmartSpark: plan, generate, check, schedule and publish content; answer comments and messages; notify the owner on WhatsApp when a human is needed; and report performance.
  • To keep SmartSpark secure and working: authentication, abuse prevention, troubleshooting and audit logs.
  • To communicate with business users about their account and the service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Our legal bases, where the law requires one, are performance of our contract with the business user, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, and legal obligation. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about individuals by automated means.

5. AI processing

SmartSpark uses AI models to write captions and replies and to create images and videos. To do this we send the minimum needed for each task: for example product facts and photos for content, or the text of a customer's message and the relevant business facts for a reply. We do not send passwords, access tokens or payment data, and we replace customer identifiers with internal references where possible.

Data sent to our AI providers is not used by them to train their models under their business API terms; they may keep it for a limited period for abuse monitoring, as their terms describe. We ask providers not to store request data where they offer that option. AI output is treated as a suggestion: prices, availability and other facts in published content and replies are checked against the data the business provided.

8. Deleting your data

You can disconnect your Instagram account, delete your organization, or ask us to delete your data at any time. If you commented on or messaged a business that uses SmartSpark, you can ask that business or us to delete your data. Full steps are on the Data Deletion Instructions page. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

9. Security

  • All traffic uses TLS. Databases and file storage are encrypted at rest.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted with a per-account key and are never written to logs.
  • Each business's data is isolated from every other business's data, and access within a business requires sign-in and membership of that business.
  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email info@smartsparkservices.com. We reply within 30 days. If your data was collected on behalf of a business (as a customer who commented or sent a message), we will pass your request to that business and help it respond. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Children

SmartSpark is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will notify business users by email before it takes effect.
