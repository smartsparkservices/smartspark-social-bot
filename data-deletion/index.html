Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 2 October 2026

You can ask SmartSpark to delete the data it holds about you at any time. Which steps apply depends on how SmartSpark came to hold your data.

If you connected your Instagram account to SmartSpark

Any one of these works:

  1. In SmartSpark: open your organization's settings, choose Social accounts, then Disconnect. We delete the stored Instagram access token immediately and stop all publishing and replying for that account.
  2. In Instagram: go to Settings and activity → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find SmartSpark and select Remove. This revokes SmartSpark's access; we treat the revocation as a disconnect.
  3. By email: write to info@smartsparkservices.com from the email address on your SmartSpark account, with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Instagram username.

Disconnecting stops access. To also erase the business data, content, comments and messages linked to the account, either delete your organization in SmartSpark (Settings → Delete organization) or ask us by email. We complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email. Copies in encrypted backups expire within a further 35 days.

If you commented on or messaged a business that uses SmartSpark

SmartSpark processes your comment or message on behalf of that business. You can:

  • ask the business directly to delete your conversation; or
  • email info@smartsparkservices.com with the subject "Data deletion request", the business's Instagram username and your own Instagram username.

We delete the stored copies of your comments and messages and your Instagram identifier for that business within 30 days, and tell the business. Deleting our copy does not remove the comment or message from Instagram itself; you can delete those in the Instagram app.

What is deleted

Everything described in the Privacy Policy for the relevant account or person, except records we must keep by law (for example, billing records), which we keep only for the period the law requires and do not use for anything else.
