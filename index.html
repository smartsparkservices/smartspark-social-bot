SmartSpark

SmartSpark helps small businesses run their Instagram marketing. A business adds its products, real product photos, facts and brand rules. SmartSpark plans posts, generates captions, images and videos, publishes them to the business's own Instagram professional account, and answers comments and direct messages using only the facts the business has provided. Anything it cannot answer is handed to the business owner.
